Projects

  • Cognitive digital twins in mental health2026 – now

    With Prof. Monojit Choudhury

    • A systematic survey of how the mental health literature conceptualizes and operationalizes cognitive digital twins.
    • A six-branch taxonomy of how digital twins are operationalized in mental health research.
    • Screened and coded 104 papers against the taxonomy to identify gaps and open challenges.
  • Digital instrument preservation2026 – now

    With Prof. Monojit Choudhury and Dr. Pravina Manoharan

    • A framework for measuring instrument fidelity in AI-generated music, using signal-processing metrics for timbre and pitch.
    • A music Turing test across listener expertise levels.
    • Models keep Western instruments more faithfully than ones like sitar and sarangi, and their output is largely distinguishable from human performance.
  • Music generation for the Global South2024 – 2026

    With Shivam Chauhan and Prof. Monojit Choudhury

    • Adapter-based fine-tuning for low-resource music traditions.
    • Representational-bias audits of music datasets and generation models.
    • Fairness frameworks and metrics for automatic music evaluation.
  • Honorifics and socio-cultural norms2025 – 2026

    With Prof. Monojit Choudhury and Dr. Akhil Arora

    • Extended our EMNLP 2025 study of third-person honorifics in Wikipedia from Hindi and Bengali to 8 South Asian languages.
    • Factor analysis of how age, gender, fame and cultural origin drive honorific choice; preference analysis to evaluate LLMs.
    • Honorifics favour older and positively famous people; gender bias against women is strongest in Hindi; LLMs match Wikipedia only in some settings and languages.
  • Keystroke dynamics for typing behaviour modelling2024 – 2026

    With Dr. Rajesh Kumar and Dr. Rajiv Ratn Shah

    • Models typing behaviour as a signature of the writing process, to tell genuine composition from LLM-assisted writing.
    • Keystroke datasets from 124 participants across writing, transcription and paraphrasing, plus a forged-keystroke attack set that mimics user-specific typing.
    • Gradient boosting and a Siamese LSTM (TypeNet) benchmarked against DetectGPT, LLaMA-3.3-70B and 44 human evaluators.
    • Over 97% F1 where text detectors and humans were near chance; adversarial training cut attack-induced false rejections from ~95% to 0%.
  • Writing style imitation2026 – now

    With Dr. Tatsuki Kuribayashi and Prof. Preslav Nakov

    • LLM simulations of individual users' writing style on PersonaConvBench (Reddit).
    • Compares in-context learning (profile-based and few-shot), LoRA fine-tuning and preference-based RL on Qwen3-8B.
    • A contrastive BERT authorship model (400 authors, 4 domains, 89% macro-F1), with lexical markers, perplexity and AI detection, measures how closely each simulation matches its user.
    • Investigating activation steering vectors as an efficient alternative to fine-tuning for per-user simulation.
